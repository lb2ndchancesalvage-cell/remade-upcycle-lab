Source

Rescue furniture, décor, fixtures, hardware, vintage pieces and usable materials that would otherwise be discarded.

Transform

Clean, repair, redesign, combine or repurpose the piece while keeping useful character and reducing waste.

Sell

Photograph the finished work, explain the transformation, price the labor and materials, and sell locally or online.

The business is the transformation.
The value is not only in the original object. It comes from sourcing, creative decisions, labor, materials, presentation and the story of giving something another life.

Problem

AI workflow
